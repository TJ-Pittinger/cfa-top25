Privacy

CFATop25.com runs the College Football Addiction Top 25, a weekly college football poll. This page explains what we collect when you sign in and vote, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • From your Google sign-in: your name, email address, and profile picture link. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Your ballots: the 25 teams you rank each week and when you submitted them.

How it's used

  • To make sure each person casts one ballot per week, and to add up the poll results.
  • Fan ballots are private. Only the combined totals are published. Your name and your individual rankings are never shown publicly. CFA admins can see fan ballots, with the name and email you signed in with, so they can remove fake or trolling ballots.
  • Media ballots are public. Invited media voters' rankings are published with their name and outlet, like the AP poll.
  • Sunday reminders: if you're a media voter, or you've voted in the Fan Poll before, we may email you a reminder on Sunday mornings when voting opens. Every reminder has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Sign-in and ballots are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Sign-in is handled by Google. Team logos and game results come from public sports data.

Your choices

  • Stop reminder emails: click Unsubscribe at the bottom of any reminder email.
  • Delete your account: sign in and go to Your account (or click your name at the top of the site), then choose Delete my account. This deletes your sign-in, your name and email, and your ballot for the week that's open. Ballots from finished weeks stay in those weeks' totals so published polls don't change, but they're no longer linked to you.
  • Remove Google access: open your Google Account's Third-party connections page, pick CFA Top 25, and remove it.
